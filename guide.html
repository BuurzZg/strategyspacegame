Play

What this is

You wake on the bridge of the Forge, a kilometre-long foundry ship stranded in a galaxy of scrap, with no memory of who you are. You cross the star chart place by place, keep your people fed, watered and breathing, fight what you have to, and piece together what happened. When you die, you wake again in the same galaxy: what you remembered stays, almost everything else is lost. You are meant to die. The longer a life lasts, the louder the thing that hunts you gets, and a whole campaign takes several lives (about six on a full galaxy). Lanes you have flown before are quick to fly again, so each life gets further, faster.

It's an early build. Balance isn't final (that's the next milestone), and some things will be rough. That's why we need you.

Before you start

  • Play on a desktop or laptop with a mouse (a scroll wheel and right button matter). Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all work. Phones run it, but it's not made for them yet.
  • Turn the sound on. It starts after your first click.
  • Your campaign saves by itself in this browser. Clearing your browser's site data, or a private window, loses it.
  • For your first life, leave Guide me through my first life ticked. Hint cards explain each screen the first time you see it.
  • Press H any time on a map for the full list of controls.

What to test

Just play, as long as you like. We especially want to hear about:

  1. The first hour. Did you understand what to do after waking? Which hint or screen confused you?
  2. The star chart. Can you read it: the wax seals, the inked lanes, the dashed hazard lanes, the pencil clue circles? Could you plan a route?
  3. Staying alive. Water, food, air, Glim and the boilers are the brass gauges at the top. Did a shortage surprise you, or feel unfair?
  4. Fights on maps. Selecting, moving in 3D, building ships, leaving with the Skein. What felt clumsy?
  5. Dying and waking. Tell us how dying felt: fair or frustrating? Did you want to try again? Was it clear what you kept (memories, the chart) and what you lost? Did the next life feel like progress?
  6. The Loom, the bridge and the deck plan. Did you find them? Did they make sense?
  7. Talking to factions and traders (the telegraph cards).
  8. Speed. If it stutters, press F8 right then: the report records your frame rate and graphics card. Tell us what was happening (a big fight, the chart, opening a screen).
  9. Anything that looks wrong: text you can't read, things overlapping, a ship flying through something, a screen that won't close.

How far you got matters too: what life, what day, and which region (the Drift, the Middens, the Hush, the Shore).

How to report a bug

  1. When something goes wrong, press F8 right away (or Report a bug on the title screen).
  2. Write what happened and what you expected. A sentence each is enough.
  3. Leave Include my save ticked: it lets us replay your game exactly up to that moment.
  4. Press Copy the report and paste it into a message, or Save it as a file and attach it.
  5. Send it to whoever gave you the game, by Discord, email or WhatsApp, whichever you use with them. A screenshot helps too.

The report holds the build, your browser and screen size, your graphics card and how fast the game has been running (the average and lowest frames per second over the last few minutes), what the game was doing (seed, life, day, place), its last error messages, and your save. Nothing is sent anywhere by itself: you choose what to share.

For things that aren't bugs (ideas, "this was boring", "this was great") just write to us. That's as useful as a bug.

Known rough edges

  • The balance across lives is a first pass, set by a test bot: a life of an hour or two, a full campaign of about 9 to 13 hours (a small galaxy 5 to 9). Tell us if lives felt too short, too long, or unfair.
  • Very big fights can be slow on older computers: tell us if it stutters, and on what.
  • Saves from older builds should load. If yours doesn't, start a new campaign and tell us.
